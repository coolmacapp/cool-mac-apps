# Contributing to Cool Mac Apps

Thanks for helping keep this curated macOS app list useful and accurate. We welcome app suggestions, factual corrections, and improvements to descriptions.

## Suggest an app

Open a [Suggest an App issue](../../issues/new?template=suggest-app.yml) with:

- **App name**
- **Official website URL**
- **Why it deserves consideration**

We review suggestions before including an app. A suggestion is not a promise of publication. The list is maintained directly in this repository.

## Report an issue

Open a [Report an Issue](../../issues/new?template=report-issue.yml) for discontinued products, broken links, inaccurate descriptions, incorrect categories, or other errors. Link to the relevant app when possible.

## Improve an entry with a pull request

Changes to `README.md` are welcome. Please:

1. Place the app under its **primary category only**.
2. Keep categories **alphabetical** and omit empty categories.
3. Keep app entries **alphabetical (A–Z)** within each category.
4. Write a **single, factual description** of approximately **100–180 characters**. Don't copy marketing taglines or make unverifiable claims.
5. Include the app's **official website** link. If the app has a page on Cool Mac Apps, you may also include an optional **Read more** link to `https://coolmac.app/apps/<slug>`; do not require or invent a slug.
6. Keep the format uniform, avoid icons/pricing/hardware badges, and update the category table when adding, removing, or renaming a category.
7. Make only relevant changes. Explain any new apps or factual changes in the PR description.

**Example syntax** (illustrative only; do not paste it as a real listing):

```md
### Category Name

- **Example App** - A one-sentence summary of the application's actual purpose and notable capabilities, based on reliable information.  
  [Official website](https://example.com) · [Read more](https://coolmac.app/apps/example-app) <!-- optional -->
```

Omit the **Read more** link when the app does not have a matching Cool Mac Apps page.

Please don't add an app solely because it's popular or paid. We prioritize useful, well-made software and clear editorial descriptions.

## Quality and attribution

See [EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md](EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md) for the writing and verification checklist. Do not copy developers' descriptions verbatim, submit paid placements disguised as editorial recommendations, or imply that every app was personally tested.

By contributing **original editorial text**, you agree it may be distributed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT.md), with attribution. Do not submit content you lack permission to license. Future original automation scripts, if any, may be contributed under [MIT](LICENSE-CODE).

## Review process

A maintainer reviews issues and pull requests. Content may be edited for clarity and consistency. Accepted app changes become part of the repository's curated list and should follow the standards above.
